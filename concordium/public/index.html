Delivery proof checks for escrow orders

This service checks the proof a worker submits for a JaraWork order (a tracking number, a link or an IPFS hash) and answers pass or fail with a short reason.

What it checks

  • Links: a real request checks that a secure, public address responds. Internal addresses are refused. It does not confirm what the page shows
  • IPFS hashes: well-formed identifiers, then a real retrieval through public gateways, which often rate-limit servers (then it answers "try later" and is not charged)
  • Tracking numbers: format checked for real; carrier status is a simulated stub and says so
  • Free text: never auto-approved
