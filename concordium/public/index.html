Delivery proof checks for escrow orders

This service checks the proof a worker submits for a JaraWork order (a tracking number, a link or an IPFS hash) and answers pass or fail with a short reason.

What it checks

  • Links: secure, public web addresses only
  • IPFS hashes: well-formed content identifiers
  • Tracking numbers: format only. It does not confirm delivery
  • Free text: never auto-approved
